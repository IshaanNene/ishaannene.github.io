## Idea

Process farm sensor data at the source, on fog nodes rather than a distant cloud, so recommendations stay real-time and work where connectivity is poor.

## Approach

- **Sensor data fusion:** soil moisture, temperature, humidity and nutrient readings combined into one picture of the field.
- **Specialised multimodal LLMs**, including one for soil nutrition and fertilisation, each focused on one part of crop management.
- **Prompt-based interaction:** farmers ask questions such as how much water the crop needs this week and get actionable answers.

The aim is healthier crops, less wasted water and fertiliser, and better yield predictions.
